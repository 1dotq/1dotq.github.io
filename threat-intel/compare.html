03 / INTERPRETATION

Read the fields carefully

KEV

Inclusion means CISA considers the vulnerability known exploited. It says nothing about how many systems are affected or attacked today.

CVSS

A technical severity rating from a source record. It is not a measurement of exploitation and may be missing or updated.

EPSS

A model estimate of exploitation probability over the next 30 days. It is not a count of observed attacks.

CISA federal due dates are shown for context. Validate versions and current fixes with the original vendor advisories.
