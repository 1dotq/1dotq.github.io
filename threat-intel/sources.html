03 / READING THE DATA

Interpretation guide

What does “last success” mean?

The collector received and processed a source response at that time. It does not mean the source published a new item or that every possible item was captured.

What does “last check” mean?

The latest recorded collection attempt. If this is newer than last success and status needs attention, inspect the original source for current information.

Does KEV rank attack volume?

No. A KEV entry establishes CISA catalog inclusion, not exploitation frequency, severity, product quality, or your exposure.

Are CVSS and EPSS interchangeable?

No. CVSS expresses technical severity in its scoring framework. EPSS estimates near-term exploitation probability. Neither is a count of observed attacks.

How often does this site update?

The public export is scheduled regularly, but it is a snapshot and can lag source updates or fail. Each page shows collection timestamps so you can assess freshness.

Where should I verify a claim?

Open the original source link and the vendor advisory. A summarized or cached item should not be your only basis for operational action.

Scope and privacy

This GitHub Pages site serves public snapshots. The editorial queue and publishing workbench stay on the internal server. A browser-local watchlist stays in your browser storage.
